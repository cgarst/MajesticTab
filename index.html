One Practice Session

Four ways MajesticTab helps you practice

Read

Show all your guitar tracks together in scroll or page mode. Open Guitar Pro, PDF, and text tabs.

Hear

Play each track with SoundFonts, set its volume in the mixer, or play a YouTube backing track.

See

Follow each note on a virtual fretboard, with a color for every track.

Play together

Host a Jam session on your local network so every player sees the same song at the same position.

Multi-Track Focus

All your guitar tracks in one view

MajesticTab shows your guitar parts together and hides drums, bass, keys, and vocals. You don't need to switch tracks while you play through a song. The layout follows the style of a printed tab book.

All guitars at once. Rhythm and lead stack together, so the whole arrangement is in front of you.
No clutter. Drums, bass, keys, and vocals are hidden.
Empty staves hidden. When a guitar rests, its staff disappears from that group, so you turn fewer pages.
Tab-book layout. Clean margins and tightly grouped staves, as in a printed tab book.

Two Ways to Read

Continuous scroll or book-like pages

Switch modes at any time, on any file. On a touch screen, pinch to resize the sheet.

Scroll (continuous flow)

The score scrolls vertically and shows your progress. Use it to learn a song section by section.

Page (spread view)

The score reads like a book. You see one page on a phone and two side by side on a wide screen. You can advance one page or two at a time.

Line cursor. A line follows playback so you can find your place at a glance.
7- and 8-string tabs. Re-finger a tab for a 7- or 8-string guitar. Every note keeps its pitch.
Remembers your setup. MajesticTab saves your hidden tracks for each tab file.

See the Notes

Follow each note on a fretboard

While a Guitar Pro tab plays, the virtual fretboard lights up the notes in the current beat. Press F to show or hide it.

A color for each track. When you show more than one track, each track's notes use its own color.
Left-handed view. Flip the neck for left-handed players.
Fits your screen. The fretboard scales to the available width instead of scrolling.
Shows the tuning. A badge lists the tuning and capo for the tracks you can see.

Hear Every Track

Play along with SoundFonts or YouTube

The built-in SoundFont synth plays the tab without an internet connection. A YouTube panel plays the original recording or a backing track above the tab.

Choose the SoundFont synth or link a YouTube backing track.

Practice at your own tempo

Your own SoundFonts. Load SF2 or SF3 files and layer them. If a SoundFont lacks an instrument, MajesticTab uses the closest match from the others. MajesticTab stores the files on your device and includes them in backups.
Per-track mixer. Set the volume of each track, plus a master volume. Press G to switch between the full band and no guitars.
Speed control. Slow the SoundFont synth to 0.75x or 0.5x to learn a solo, then speed it up as you improve.
Latency calibration. If the sound lags behind the cursor, tap along to measure your device's audio delay and compensate for it.
YouTube backing tracks. MajesticTab searches YouTube for the original recording or a backing track. You can edit the search and try again.
10-second rewind. Select rewind, or press B, to repeat the last few bars.

Play Together

Jam on the same page, on your local network

One person hosts a session in a native MajesticTab app. Everyone else joins from the app or from a web browser on the same network. Playback position and YouTube stay in sync.

Join with a QR code. Each host address has a copy button and a QR code, so players can join from a phone.
Share any song type. Guitar Pro, PDF, and text tabs all open for every player.
Assign tracks. As host, give each guitarist a track. Options let you play only assigned tracks or only the backing tracks.
Control the audio. Mute audio on guests, or mute assigned tracks for people who have no assignment.

Hosting needs a native app.

Tune and Learn

Tuner, tuning adapter, and chord library

Check your tuning, fit a tab to your guitar, and look up chords and scales without leaving the app.

Chromatic tuner. The tuner listens through your microphone. When you have a tab open, it offers the tunings that tab uses.
Adapt to My Tuning. Choose your own tuning and MajesticTab re-fingers the tab by shifting frets, or by adding a capo, while keeping the pitch of each note.
Chord library. Browse chord voicings by root note and family. Turn on Prefer Bar Chords to change the default voicings.
Scales and circle of fifths. View scales on the fretboard, invert or flip them for left-handed play, and open chords from the circle of fifths.

Library and Your Data

A tab library that organizes itself

Add Guitar Pro and PDF tabs to MajesticTab. The library groups songs by artist and album, adds MusicBrainz metadata, and backs everything up in one file.

MusicBrainz Metadata. MajesticTab fetches artist discographies, album covers, release years, and track orders.
Custom Collections. Group tabs into playlists, live sets, tuning categories such as Drop D or Eb Standard, or exercises.
One-file backup. Export or restore your library, settings, extensions, and SoundFonts in one .mtbackup file.
Recents and search. Filter songs by title, artist, or album as you type. Reopen the tab you used last.
Swipe to go back. Swipe, or use the back and forward buttons on your mouse, to move through the library.
Links that stay put. The library, recents, tuner, and reference pages each have their own address, so the browser back button works.
Export in bulk. Save every PDF in your library in one step.

Built for Practice

Less setup, more playing

Notation editors are made for writing music. MajesticTab is made for reading and practicing it, with extra tools for PDF tabs.

Open and go

Pick a PDF, a Guitar Pro file (GP, GPX, GP5, GP4, GP3), or a text file. You don't need an account or a project.

Fills your screen

MajesticTab trims margins and scales the notation to your display, so you see more music per screen.

Export to PDF

Save what you see as a printable A4 PDF to print, share with bandmates, or open on another device.

Condense PDF EXPERIMENTAL

For multi-track tab PDFs, MajesticTab finds the staves that have notes, drops the empty ones, and redraws the groups to shorten the PDF.

Bookmarks. Use the table of contents to jump to a song in a PDF tab.
Page ranges. Map a song in a multi-song PDF to its pages. The reader and bookmarks show only that range.
Landscape page mode. Show PDF pages side by side on a wide screen.

Hands-Free Control

Gamepad and Bluetooth page turner support

Keep both hands on your instrument. Navigate scores, turn pages, and practice seamlessly with game controllers, foot pedals, or your handheld Steam Deck.

Controllers, Foot Pedals & Steam Deck

MajesticTab connects directly to the W3C Gamepad API with full native support for Xbox controllers, DualShock/DualSense, 8BitDo gamepads, and Steam Deck handhelds, as well as Bluetooth foot pedals (like AirTurn, PageFlip, and Donner).
