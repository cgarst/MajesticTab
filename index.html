MajesticTab is a distraction-free viewer and manager for guitar tabs. See every guitar track in one clean multi-track view, manage your entire song collection in the built-in library, navigate with foot pedals or game controllers, and play along with SoundFont synth or YouTube backing tracks.

Multi-Track Focus

Every guitar track. Nothing else.

See all your guitar parts together in one multi-track view, without drums, bass, keys or vocals cluttering the page. No more switching between tracks while you play through a song. It reads like a professional tab book.

All guitars at once. Rhythm and lead stack together, so the whole arrangement is in front of you.
No clutter. Drums, bass, keys and vocals are left out of the view.
Empty staves hidden. When a guitar rests, its staff disappears from that group, so every line shows only what is playing and you turn fewer pages.
Tab-book layout. Clean margins and tightly grouped staves, like the printed books.

Two Ways to Read

Continuous scroll or book-like pages

Switch with one tap, any time, on any file.

Scroll (Continuous Flow)

Endless vertical flow with a progress indicator. Ideal for learning a song section by section, or when you'd rather not break the flow.

Page (Spread View)

Like a real book. One page on a phone, two side by side on a wide screen. Choose to advance one page or two at a time.

Tab Library

Built-in tab library. Organized automatically.

Drop hundreds of Guitar Pro and PDF tab files into MajesticTab. Your songs are cataloged by artist and album, enhanced with MusicBrainz metadata, and backed up in a single file.

MusicBrainz Metadata. Automatic artist discographies, album covers, release years, and track orders fetched automatically.
Custom Collections. Group tabs by custom playlists, live sets, tuning categories (Drop D, 7-string, Eb Standard), or exercises.
Google Drive & Single-File Backup. Export or restore your entire library, settings, and custom extensions in a single portable .mtbackup bundle.
Recents & Instant Filter. Filter hundreds of songs by title, artist, or album in real time. Pick up right where you left off.

Bring Your Own Data

Your music, your privacy, your choice.

No mandatory subscriptions, no proprietary cloud locks, and no walled gardens. MajesticTab puts you in complete control with three flexible file providers for opening, organizing, and backing up your tab collection.

Local Device

Offline-First

Direct disk and filesystem access

Open and store tabs directly from your computer, SSD, phone, or tablet. Supports Guitar Pro (.gp, .gp3–.gp5, .gpx), text tabs, and PDFs. Tabs remain 100% on your device with zero cloud dependency.

Fully private • Instant local load • Offline ready

Google Drive

Cloud Sync

Personal cloud storage without middleman servers

Connect your personal Google Drive account via direct client-side OAuth. Browse folders, open scores seamlessly across desktop, laptop, and mobile devices, and save full-library .mtbackup archives directly to your cloud drive.

Direct Google OAuth • Cross-device access • Cloud backups

Tab Downloader

Collect Tabs Fast

Integrated online tab search engine

Find and download tabs directly from public guitar tab web archives without leaving the app. Downloaded tabs are automatically cataloged into your library, complete with album metadata, tunings, and track variations.

Native client only • 1-click search • Instant library import

Practice in Sync

YouTube audio and backing tracks

Stream original recordings and backing tracks right above your tab. Speed up, slow down, or loop tricky sections without juggling multiple apps.

Play along with the masters

MajesticTab automatically finds original or backing tracks from YouTube to keep your eyes on the sheet. Or, use SoundFonts to practice at your own tempo.

Smart Backing Track Search: Automatically queries for isolated guitar backing tracks or official audio matching your song title.
Tempo & Speed Controls: Slow SoundFonts down to 0.75x or 0.5x to practice intricate solos and speed up as you get comfortable.
Instant 10-Second Rewind: One tap on the top bar jumps back 10 seconds so you can repeat difficult bars without losing your flow.
Offline SoundFont MIDI Synth: No internet? Use the built-in multi-track SoundFont synthesizer to hear the tab playback offline.

Hands-Free Control

Gamepad and Bluetooth page turner support

Keep both hands on your instrument. Navigate scores, turn pages, and practice seamlessly with game controllers, foot pedals, or your handheld Steam Deck.

Controllers, Foot Pedals & Steam Deck

MajesticTab connects directly to the W3C Gamepad API with full native support for Xbox controllers, DualShock/DualSense, 8BitDo gamepads, and Steam Deck handhelds, as well as Bluetooth foot pedals (like AirTurn, PageFlip, and Donner).

Gamepad & Handheld
  • LB / RB Page Turn
  • LT / RT Analog Scroll
  • Y Zen Mode (Hide HUD)
  • A / B Play/Pause & Back
Bluetooth Foot Pedals
  • ← / → Prev / Next Page
  • PgUp / PgDn Prev / Next Page
  • Space Play / Pause

Interactive controller guide overlay accessible anytime with your controller or keyboard shortcuts.
