Sync a tab to a recording, and the cursor follows the music. You can use a YouTube video or an audio file on your device.

The original recording panel.

Add a recording

  1. In the dock, select Original. If you haven't added a recording, its settings open.
  2. For an audio file on your device, select Add audio file and choose the file.
  3. For YouTube, enter a link or video ID under Add a video, or search by song. A link or ID is added as you entered it. A search adds the first match. Select Add.

The list Recordings for this tab shows everything you added: audio files first, then videos. A Guitar Pro file that carries its own audio adds a row at the top, Audio in the Guitar Pro file. Select a row to use it as the sound. Each recording keeps its own sync points. Select Remove recordings… to remove recordings and their sync points. Audio files stay on your device. They're included in backups and cloud sync unless you turn that off.

Sync the recording by tapping

The recording bar above the bar ruler shows the sync state of the recording: Not synced, Partly synced, Synced or Check bar with a bar number. Everything you do to sync a recording starts there.

  1. In the recording bar, select Sync. On a Guitar Pro tab, the Sync sheet opens.
  2. In the Sync sheet, select Tap along.
  3. Play the recording. Tap each bar of the tab when you hear it.
  4. Select Done. The Sync sheet opens again and tells you whether the recording is synced, and which bars to fix if it is not.

On a PDF or text tab, Sync starts tapping straight away, and the recording bar has the controls for it.

After you sync, the cursor follows the recording through tempo changes. The metronome and count-in follow it too. Syncing works for Guitar Pro, PDF and text tabs.

The tap controls show play, back 5 and 10 seconds, speed, Undo and Done. More opens Fix range, the tap Offset, Calibrate, Snap for an audio file, Redo, Timeline and Edit points. Calibrate measures how long you take to tap after you hear a sound, and removes that delay from every tap.

Correct sync points

When a recording has sync points, the recording bar shows Edit sync instead of Sync. Select it to open the Sync sheet. It tells you what looks wrong: a bar whose point looks out of place, or bars with no point. From the sheet you can:

  • Select Tap along again to tap over the bars that drift. In tap mode, select More and then Fix range to redo a stretch of bars without redoing the rest.
  • Select Edit points to open the points. There you can nudge a point, play two seconds around it to check it, move it to the nearest sound in the recording, shift every point at once, or delete a point.

Reuse sync points

In the Sync sheet, select Use another recording’s sync to copy the points of another video or file of the same tab. If the other recording is an audio file and so is this one, MajesticTab lines them up by the sound. Otherwise it copies the times as they are. Then the points open so that you can shift them by ear. If this recording already has points, you select the other recording twice to replace them.

Select Use another tab’s sync to copy a synced video from another tab of the same song. Select Import points… to load points from a file or an address.

Add a backing track

Switch the panel from Recording to Backing to attach a separate backing track to the same tab. Sync it the same way. Then you can switch between the original recording and the backing track. To make the backing track follow the original recording’s points, turn on Use the original recording’s sync in the Sync sheet.

Change the speed

The original recording has its own speed options: 50%, 75%, 100% and 125%.

Use a recording in a Jam

Guests don't hear a local audio file from the host. They follow the host's YouTube video or the synth. See Jam sessions.
