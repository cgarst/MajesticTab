Scales shows a scale or mode on a fretboard, so you can see where its notes fall on the neck.

Open scales

  • In the library, select Tools and then Scales.
  • In the reader, select Tools and then Scales, or press S.

Show a scale on the neck

  1. Select a Root note.
  2. Select a Scale or mode, for example Major Pentatonic or Minor Pentatonic.
  3. Set the Strings and the Tuning to match your guitar.

The Interactive Scale Fretboard marks the notes of the scale. The root note is marked in the main text color. Scale degrees 2 to 7 each take one of the six guitar track colors. The Formula shows how the scale is built.
