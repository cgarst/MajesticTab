Practice contains the controls for learning a part slowly and bringing it up to tempo.

Change the speed

Set the playback speed from 50% to 125%. Select a preset (50%, 75%, 90%, 100%, 110% or 125%) or use the buttons to change the speed in steps of 1%. On a keyboard, press , to slow down and . to speed up, in steps of 5%. The pitch stays the same. This includes a local audio file that you linked as the original recording.

Loop bars

You can repeat part of a song in two ways:

  • Under Sections, select a section to loop it.
  • Under Loop bars, enter a From bar and a To bar.

Use the metronome and count-in

To turn on the metronome and the count-in, select the Metronome and Count-in switches in Practice. You can also press M and I. When you sync the original recording, the metronome and count-in follow it through tempo changes.

Choose the parts to play

Under Audio Tracks, select Full Band, Guitars Only or No Guitars. No Guitars plays the other instruments so you can play the guitar part yourself. To switch between full band and no guitars from a keyboard, press G. In bass mode, the same key switches between full band and no bass.

Rewind

To go back 10 seconds, select Back 10 Seconds in the dock, press B, or press X on a game controller.
