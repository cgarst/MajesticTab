This page lists the file types that MajesticTab opens and creates.

Tab files you can open

MajesticTab opens these types of tab:

TypeExtensions
Guitar Pro.gp, .gp3, .gp4, .gp5, .gpx
PDF.pdf
Text tab.txt

Audio

  • For the original recording, you can use a YouTube video or an audio file on your device.
  • For the synth, you can add SoundFonts in .sf2 and .sf3 format.

Files MajesticTab creates

FileContents
.mtbackupYour library, settings and SoundFonts
.mtabA single song
.pdfOne exported tab. Bulk Export saves many PDFs in one ZIP archive.
