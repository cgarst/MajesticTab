You can turn pages and control playback without touching the screen. MajesticTab supports Bluetooth foot pedals and game controllers.

Use a Bluetooth foot pedal

MajesticTab works with AirTurn, PageFlip and Donner pedals. Most page-turn pedals connect as a Bluetooth keyboard and send arrow or page keys. MajesticTab treats those keys like the keys in Keyboard shortcuts. Pair the pedal in your device's Bluetooth settings. Then open a tab and press the pedal.

Use a game controller

MajesticTab supports Xbox, PlayStation and Steam Deck controllers. To see the controller diagram, open Settings, select Controls, and then select Game Controller. While a score is open, the controls work as follows:

ControlAction
APlay or pause
XGo back 10 seconds, or one measure
BReturn to the library
YToggle Reading mode
LB or LTPrevious page
RB or RTNext page
Left stickScroll smoothly
Right stickZoom in and out
Left or right stick clickChange the view mode, or reset the zoom to 100%

In the library and in menus, the controls work as follows:

ControlAction
ASelect, or open the selected tab
BGo back, or close a dialog
XFocus the search field
YShow or hide the top bar
D-pad or left stickMove the focus
Right stick, verticalScroll a list
LB or RBPrevious or next library tab
View buttonCycle the audio. Hold to show the controller guide.
