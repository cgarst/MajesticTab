The tuner listens to your guitar through the microphone and shows how far each note is from the pitch you want.

Tune your guitar

  1. Open the tuner. In the library, select Tools and then Tuner. In the reader, select Tools and then Tuner, or press T.
  2. Select Start tuner.
  3. If your device asks, allow microphone access. The tuner needs the microphone to listen to your instrument.
  4. Play one string. The tuner shows the note and whether it is sharp or flat, in cents.

Before you play, the tuner shows Waiting for audio. If the microphone isn't available, you see Microphone access failed.

Choose what to tune

  • Choose a number of Strings.
  • Choose one of your Library tunings.
  • While a tab is open, choose the Active track to tune to that track's tuning.
  • Choose chromatic mode to detect every note on any instrument. It detects every note in any pitch range.

Hear a reference note

Select Play a note to hear the pitch you are tuning to.

Set the reference pitch

To change the pitch that the tuner uses for A4, use the Reference pitch for A4 control.
