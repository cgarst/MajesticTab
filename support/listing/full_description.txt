MajesticTab is a guitar tab reader and practice app. It opens Guitar Pro files (.gp, .gp3, .gp4, .gp5, .gpx), PDFs and text tabs, keeps them in a library built around the songs you play, and works offline. No ads, no account, no subscription and no in-app purchases.

A library, not a folder of files
• Link your tabs to the songs you play. Artist discographies, album covers, release years and track orders are filled in for you.
• Browse by artist or by tuning (Standard, Drop D, DADGAD, 7-string, 8-string and your own), and pin the songs you play most.
• Build setlists for gigs and rehearsals, and group tabs into your own collections.
• Back up your library, settings and SoundFonts in one file, or keep them in sync through cloud storage you choose.

Read every guitar part together
• Guitar tracks stack together while drums, bass, keys and vocals stay out of the way. MajesticTab remembers your tracks for each file.
• Scroll continuously, or turn book-like pages in a single page or a two-page spread.
• Turn pages hands-free with a Bluetooth foot pedal (AirTurn, PageFlip, Donner) or a game controller.
• Condense multi-track tab PDFs by removing empty staves and margins, and export your whole library to PDF in one step.
• Choose an app theme and paper color that are easy on the eyes.

Play along with the original recording
• Tap to sync any tab to the record you learned it from, from YouTube or an audio file on your device, and the cursor follows the music through tempo changes. Attach a backing track and sync it the same way.
• The built-in offline SoundFont synth (load your own SF2 or SF3 files) has a mixer for every track, speed from 50% to 125%, loop for the bars you are learning, count-in, metronome and a 10-second rewind.
• A virtual fretboard lights up the notes of the current beat, with a color for each track and a left-handed option.

Fit any tab to your guitar
• Show a Guitar Pro tab on 7 or 8 strings, or move the frets to match your tuning, with a capo or transposed, and every note keeps its pitch. It applies to the song you have open. Save it for that song, or as your usual guitar, only when you want to.

Tools and jam sessions
• A chord library for any tuning and capo, scales, the circle of fifths and a microphone tuner.
• Host or join a Jam on your local network so everyone in the room stays on the same bar. Players join from their own device with a QR code, and each guitarist can have their own part.
