Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

This policy covers MajesticTab on every platform: the desktop (Windows, macOS, Linux), Android, iPhone and iPad apps, however you install them. MajesticTab runs no servers and has no accounts, so the developer does not collect your data. Your sheet music and practice materials stay with you. A few optional features talk to other services, and this page lists every one of them.

1. What stays on your device

Opening, parsing, rendering and playing sheet music, PDFs, Guitar Pro files (.gp, .gp3, .gp4, .gp5, .gpx) and plain text tabs happens on your device. MajesticTab does not upload your files to any server run by the developer, and has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Nothing leaves your device unless you turn on a feature below that sends it, and in every case it goes to a service you chose or to the people you share with, never to the developer.

2. What is stored on your device

MajesticTab keeps its data in the app's own storage. This includes:

  • Your library: song files you save, tags, artwork, setlists and play history
  • Appearance, playback, metronome and viewing preferences
  • File converters you add (their source code)
  • Connection details for the optional services below, including a Google sign-in refresh token and, if you set up S3 backup, your endpoint, access key ID and secret access key

Credentials are stored on your device only and are sent only to the service they belong to. They are not encrypted by MajesticTab, so anyone with access to your unlocked device could read them. Use a key limited to one bucket. You can remove stored data at any time by disconnecting a service in the app, or uninstalling the app.

3. Backup and sync services you choose

These are optional. Each sends your data only to the account or storage you set up.

  • Google Drive. The app requests the drive.file permission only. It can access just the files you pick in the Google Drive Picker and the files and folder it creates itself (such as backups). It cannot see or browse the rest of your Drive. Sign-in goes through Google, and the app keeps a refresh token on your device.
  • S3-compatible storage. Backups go to the bucket and endpoint you enter, using your keys. The provider you choose has its own privacy policy.
  • iCloud Drive (Apple devices). Backups can be saved to your iCloud container and are handled by Apple.
  • Local folders and files. Backups and exports you save to your device or to a folder you pick.

MajesticTab's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Downloaded files and tokens are never sent to the developer.

4. Jam sessions

Jam mode lets devices play the same song together. A host advertises a session on the local network (Bonjour or multicast, where the platform supports it) and shares with connected guests the song file, playback position, settings and, if enabled, custom SoundFonts. Data travels directly between the devices in the session, not through a server. Apps ask for local network permission for this. Only host or join a session with people you trust, because they receive the files you share.

5. Online lookups and embedded services

Some features contact third parties directly from your device. Those services receive your IP address and the request itself, and apply their own policies.

  • Song and album details. Artist, album and song names from your files may be sent to MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive to find release information and cover art. Results are cached on your device.
  • YouTube backing tracks. The rehearsal player embeds YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode where available) and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and YouTube's Terms of Service. Finding a video may also send your search text to public YouTube search services, which receive it along with your IP address. MajesticTab does not collect your viewing history or credentials.
  • Search and download browser (where available). A built-in web view opens third-party websites you choose, including Google search. Those sites may set cookies and collect data under their own policies, and nothing you enter there is read by MajesticTab. Files you download are saved into your library on your device.

6. Permissions

  • Microphone (tuner). Audio is analyzed on your device to detect pitch. It is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
  • Local network (Jam). See section 4.
  • Files and folders. Used only for files and folders you pick.
  • Game controllers and Bluetooth page turners. Input is read on your device only.

7. File converters

You can optionally add your own file converters, which convert a file when you open it. Converters run in an isolated sandbox with no network access, no access to your stored data and no access to your accounts. They only receive the file being opened. MajesticTab includes no converters and does not send converter code to other devices.

8. No tracking, ads or accounts

  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking by MajesticTab.
  • No MajesticTab account or email address is needed.
  • MajesticTab itself sets no tracking cookies. Embedded third-party content (YouTube and the sites in the browser described in section 5) may set their own.
  • Any optional paid unlock in an app store is handled entirely by that store. MajesticTab does not receive your payment details.
  • MajesticTab is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version and date will appear on this page.

10. Questions and contact

If you have questions or concerns about this policy or MajesticTab's privacy practices, please open an issue on our GitHub issue tracker.
