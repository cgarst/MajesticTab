Read

All your guitar tracks in one view

MajesticTab shows your guitar parts together and hides drums, bass, keys, and vocals, so you never switch tracks mid-song. The layout follows a printed tab book.

All guitars at once. Rhythm and lead stack together, so the whole arrangement is in front of you.
No clutter, fewer page turns. Other instruments are hidden, and empty staves disappear when a guitar rests.
7- and 8-string tabs. Re-finger a tab for your guitar. Every note keeps its pitch.

Continuous scroll or book-like pages

Switch modes at any time, on any file. On a touch screen, pinch to resize the sheet.

Scroll

The score scrolls vertically and shows your progress. Use it to learn a song section by section.

Page

The score reads like a book: one page on a phone, two side by side on a wide screen.

Line cursor. A line follows playback so you can find your place at a glance.
Remembers your setup. MajesticTab saves your hidden tracks for each tab file.
Light or dark sheets. The light sheet is free. adds dark, auto, and theme-colored sheets.

A floating top bar that stays out of the way

Navigation, layout, tools, and page controls in one place.

Song title menu. Switch between Tab Only and Tab + Standard Notation, show or hide tracks, or adapt the tab to your tuning.
Tools menu. Open the chord library, fretboard, tuner, and Jam mode. Pin your favorites to the bar.
Playback dock. Play, 10-second rewind, the synth, and the YouTube panel, floating at the bottom of the score.

See

Follow each note on a fretboard

While a Guitar Pro tab plays, the virtual fretboard lights up the notes in the current beat. Press F to show or hide it.

A color for each track. With more than one track shown, each track's notes use their own color.
Left-handed view. Flip the neck for left-handed players.
Fits your screen. The fretboard scales to the available width instead of scrolling.
Shows the tuning. A badge lists the tuning and capo for the tracks you can see.

Hear

Play along with the built-in synth or YouTube

The built-in synth plays the tab with no internet connection. A YouTube panel plays the original recording or a backing track above the tab.

Choose the synth or link a YouTube backing track.

Practice at your own tempo

Per-track mixer. Set the volume of each track, plus a master volume. Press G to switch between the full band and no guitars.
Speed control. Slow the synth to 0.75x or 0.5x to learn a solo, then speed it up as you improve.
10-second rewind. Select rewind, or press B, to repeat the last few bars.
YouTube backing tracks. MajesticTab searches for the original recording or a backing track. You can edit the search and try again.
Latency calibration. If the sound lags behind the cursor, tap along to measure your device's audio delay and compensate for it.
Your own SoundFonts. Load SF2 or SF3 files and layer them. If a SoundFont lacks an instrument, MajesticTab uses the closest match from the others. The files stay on your device and are included in backups.

Play together

Jam on the same page, on your local network

One person hosts a session in a native MajesticTab app. Everyone else joins from the app on the same network. Playback position and YouTube stay in sync.

Join with a QR code. Each host address has a copy button and a QR code, so players can join from a phone.
Share any song type. Guitar Pro, PDF, and text tabs all open for every player.
Assign tracks. Give each guitarist a track. Options let you play only assigned tracks or only the backing tracks.
Control the audio. Mute audio on guests, or mute assigned tracks for people who have no assignment.

Anyone can join a Jam for free. Hosting needs a native app and is a feature in the App Store, Google Play, and Microsoft Store versions.

Also included

Everything else a practice session needs

Tuner, tuning adapter, and chord library

Check your tuning, fit a tab to your guitar, and look up chords and scales without leaving the app.

Chromatic tuner. Listens through your microphone, and offers the tunings your open tab uses.
Adapt to my tuning. Choose your tuning and MajesticTab re-fingers the tab, shifting frets or adding a capo, while keeping every pitch.
Chord library. Browse voicings by root note and family. Turn on Prefer Barre Chords to change the defaults.
Scales and circle of fifths. View scales on the fretboard, flip them for left-handed play, and open chords from the circle.

Turn pages with a foot pedal or controller

Keep both hands on your guitar. MajesticTab works with Bluetooth foot pedals (AirTurn, PageFlip, Donner), Xbox, DualShock/DualSense, and 8BitDo gamepads, and the Steam Deck.
