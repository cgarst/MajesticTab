A library, not a folder of files

Most tab apps hand you a list of files. MajesticTab builds a real library around the songs you play, so every tab has a home.

Artists, albums and tracklists

Add the songs or albums you play, then link your Guitar Pro, PDF and text tabs, audio tracks, stems and YouTube videos to them. MajesticTab fetches artist discographies, album covers, release years and track orders, so what you add matches the real releases.

Play along with the original recording

Map any tab to the record you learned it from, and the cursor follows the music.

It follows as soon as you link it

Link the original recording from YouTube, or an audio file on your device, and a Guitar Pro tab follows it right away, roughly. For an exact cursor, select Not mapped, then Tap along, and click each bar as you hear it. From then on the cursor follows the recording through tempo changes and live drift, and the metronome and count-in follow it too.

Staff-based text tabs with a linked MIDI file are aligned to it automatically. Other PDF and text tabs are not mapped on their own, but you can tap along to map their pages or lines.

YouTube·audio files·backing tracks

Fix a stretch, not the song

Re-tap a range of bars, nudge a point and audition the moment around it, or snap a tap to the nearest sound in your audio file.

A backing track too

Attach a separate backing track to the same tab and map it the same way, then switch between the original and the backing track.

Your audio stays yours

Audio files live on your device. They travel with your backups and cloud sync unless you turn that off. Export a song as an open Music Pack (.mpak) file and its score, recordings and map points stay together, in or out of the app.

Make any tab fit your guitar

Show a Guitar Pro tab on 7 or 8 strings, or move the frets to match your tuning, with a capo or transposed, and every note keeps its pitch. It applies to the song you have open and resets when you close it. Save it for that song, or as your usual guitar, only when you want to.

And the basics, done properly

Everything else you expect from a tab reader is here.

Read

Every guitar part sits on the page together, in scroll or book-like pages. Drums, bass, keys and vocals stay out of the way, and MajesticTab remembers your tracks for each file.

Hear

The built-in synth plays the tab offline, with a mixer for every track and room for your own SoundFonts.

See

The virtual fretboard lights up the notes in the current beat, with a color for each track, and flips for left-handed players.

Questions

How does pricing work?

MajesticTab is open source, and free on the Microsoft Store, Google Play, Linux and direct desktop and Android downloads (.dmg, .exe, .apk). On the Apple App Store, a one-time purchase covers iPhone, iPad and Mac, to support App Store distribution costs.

Are there subscriptions or locked features?

No. There are no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no ads and no trial clocks. Nothing is locked behind an upgrade.

Does it work offline?

Yes. It is a native app, and the built-in synth plays tabs with no connection. YouTube, web search and cloud sync need internet, and you choose whether to use them.

Which files can I open?

Guitar Pro files (.gp, .gp3 to .gp5, .gpx), PDFs, text tabs and Music Pack (.mpak) song files, from your device or from Google Drive, S3, WebDAV or iCloud.

Where are my tabs kept?

On your device, unless you choose to back them up or sync them. Connect your own Google Drive, S3, WebDAV or iCloud to keep every device in step, or save everything to one .mtbackup file.

Can I map a tab to the original recording?

Yes. Link a YouTube video or an audio file from your device, then tap along to map it bar by bar. Guitar Pro tabs get a rough map automatically. The audio stays on your device and can travel with your backups.

Can it find tabs for me?

Yes. You can search tab sites without leaving MajesticTab. Each tab you save is added to your library with its album metadata and tuning.

Can I jam with friends?

Yes, on the same network. Every MajesticTab app can host a Jam, and everyone else joins from their own app.

How does it help if I play a 7-string or 8-string guitar?

Show a Guitar Pro tab on 7 or 8 strings, or move the frets for your tuning or capo. Every note keeps its pitch.
