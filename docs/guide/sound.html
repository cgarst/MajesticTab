Sound

Sound opens the settings for the sound you selected in the dock. Select Synth for MajesticTab's built-in playback, or Original for a recording.

Use the synth

MajesticTab plays Guitar Pro tabs through a built-in SoundFont synth that works offline.

Mix the tracks

The Mixer sets the volume and pan of each track, and lets you mute or solo it. Select Reset levels to return every track to its default.

Add SoundFonts

Open SoundFonts to add your own .sf2 or .sf3 files. You can also manage them in Settings under Sound. Backups include your SoundFonts.

Use the original recording

Select Original in the dock to play a YouTube video or an audio file instead of the synth. The cursor follows the recording. See Original recording.

Compensate for audio latency

A Bluetooth speaker can play sound late. Audio latency delays the cursor, scrolling and fretboard to match. Use -10 and +10 to change the compensation in steps of 10 ms, or select Calibrate to measure it. To calibrate, start the clicks and tap each time you hear a click. After a few taps, MajesticTab shows the measured delay. Select Apply to use it.

Play bass tabs

If you play bass, set Preferred instrument to Bass under Player in Settings.
