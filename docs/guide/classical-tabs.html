Classical tab browser

The classical tab browser lets you search and open classical guitar tabs and MIDI files. The tabs come from Classtab, a free archive of classical guitar tabs. You download the archive once. After that, it works offline.

About Classtab

Classtab (classtab.org) publishes the classical guitar tabs and MIDI files that this browser shows. MajesticTab doesn't include or host them. You download the archive from classtab.org, and MajesticTab reads the pieces from the copy on your device. To learn more about the archive or its sources, visit the Classtab website.

Download the archive

The first time you open the browser, it offers the archive of about 3,800 pieces by 600 composers. Choose one of these:

  • Select Download archive. MajesticTab downloads it once from classtab.org. In the apps, the download is about 14 MB.
  • Select Use a ZIP from this device and then Choose ZIP file if you already have the archive.

If you can't download from inside the app, get the archive from classtab.org and use Use a ZIP from this device.

Open the classical tab browser

In the library, select Tools and then Classical tab browser. On the start screen of an empty library, select Explore classical guitar library.

Find a piece

  • Select a composer in the Composers list to see their pieces. On a phone, the browser shows one list at a time.
  • Type in Search composers and pieces to search both.
  • Use the filters to narrow the list. Select Clear filters to remove them.
  • Open Pinned to see the pieces you pinned.

Select a piece to open it. MajesticTab reads the piece from the stored archive.

Add a piece to your library

A piece that you added shows In your library. To add it again, select Add another copy. Pieces in your library are copies. They stay when you remove the archive.

Thank you, Classtab. Classtab gives guitarists free access to about 3,800 pieces by 600 composers, as tabs and MIDI files. That is a large body of classical repertoire that anyone can learn from, practice with and play, at no cost. Many guitarists rely on it, and we are grateful to the people who built and maintain it. If you find it useful, visit classtab.org and support their work.
