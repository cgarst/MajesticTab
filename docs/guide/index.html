MajesticTab guide

Learn how to add tabs, read and practice them, and play with other people.

New to MajesticTab? Start with Open your first tab. To find a key, see Keyboard shortcuts.

Start

  • Install MajesticTabInstall MajesticTab on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows, Android, Linux or Steam Deck.
  • Open your first tabPlay a tab, and then add it to your library.

Library

  • Your libraryOrganize tabs by artist, album and song. Pin songs and reopen recent tabs.
  • Setlists and tuningsCreate setlists and browse your library by tuning.
  • Getting tabs inOpen files from your device or cloud storage, search the web for tabs, import a folder or browse classical tabs.
  • Backup and syncBack up your library to one .mtbackup file, or sync it through Google Drive, S3, WebDAV or iCloud.

The reader

  • The readerLearn the five reader controls, the song menu, the playback dock and Reading mode.
  • ViewChoose the layout, notation, size, paper and tracks of a score.
  • PracticeChange the speed, loop bars, use the metronome and count-in, and choose which parts play.
  • SoundUse the built-in synth, the mixer and SoundFonts, and compensate for audio latency.
  • Original recordingMap a tab to a YouTube video or an audio file by tapping along.
  • Fit a tab to your guitarShow a tab on 6, 7 or 8 strings, and match your tuning, capo or transposition.
  • Fretboard and the Tools menuShow the fretboard and open the reference and practice tools from the reader.
  • PDF and text tabsCondense PDFs, bookmark pages, split a book into songs and read text tabs.

Together

  • Jam sessionsHost or join a Jam on your local network so every player is on the same bar.

Library tools

  • The Tools viewFind the reference, practice, tab and library tools in one list.
  • Chord libraryLook up chord voicings that match your strings, tuning and capo.
  • ScalesShow a scale or mode on the fretboard.
  • Circle of fifthsLook up a key, its signature, its relative minor and its chords.
  • TunerTune your guitar with the microphone.
  • MetronomeKeep time with tap tempo, time signatures and a visual click.
  • Open filePlay a tab without adding it to your library.
  • Classical tab browserSearch and open classical guitar tabs and MIDI files.
  • Fill in missing tabsSearch the web for a tab of each song that has none.
  • Bulk importImport a folder of tabs or audio files and pair each file with a song.
  • Bulk exportExport your library as a spreadsheet, map data, tab files or PDFs.

Controls

  • Keyboard shortcutsLook up the keyboard and mouse shortcuts for the reader and the library.
  • Foot pedals and game controllersTurn pages and control playback with a Bluetooth foot pedal or a game controller.

Reference

  • SettingsSet the theme, defaults, updates and data options.
  • Supported filesSee which file types MajesticTab opens and creates.
  • Troubleshooting and questionsFind answers to common questions and fixes for common problems.
