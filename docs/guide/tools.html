Fretboard and the Tools menu

The reader's Tools menu opens the practice and reference tools while a score is open. The library has the same tools, and more, in its Tools view.

Show the fretboard

On Guitar Pro tabs, the fretboard appears above the dock. It lights up the notes of the current beat, with a color for each track. To flip it for left-handed play, turn on Left-handed guitar under Player in Settings. To show or hide it, select Toggle fretboard in the dock, or press F.

Open a tool

Select Tools and then select a tool, or press its key:

ToolKeyWhat it does
Chord libraryCVoicings for any tuning and capo
ScalesSScales and modes on the neck
Circle of fifthsOKeys and their chords
TunerTTune with the microphone
MetronomeMKeep time at any tempo
Jam modeHost or join on your network
