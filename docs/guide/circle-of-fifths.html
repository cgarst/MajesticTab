Circle of fifths

The circle of fifths shows how keys relate to each other, and the chords in each key.

Open the circle of fifths

  • In the library, select Tools and then Circle of fifths.
  • In the reader, select Tools and then Circle of fifths, or press O.

Look up a key

Select a key on the circle. MajesticTab shows these details for it:

  • Key signature
  • Relative minor
  • Chords in the key of the key you selected
