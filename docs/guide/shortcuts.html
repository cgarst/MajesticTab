Keyboard shortcuts

Keyboard shortcuts work while a score is open and no text field has focus. The same list is in the app. Open Settings and select Controls.

Control playback

These keys control playback and the setlist:

KeyAction
SpacePlay or pause. If no audio is loaded, go to the next page.
BGo back 10 seconds
GSwitch between full band and no guitars (no bass, in bass mode)
MTurn the metronome on or off
ITurn the count-in on or off
, .Slow down or speed up by 5%
[ ]Open the previous or next tab in the open setlist

Open panels

These keys open and close panels:

KeyAction
FShow or hide the fretboard
TShow or hide the tuner
CShow or hide the chord library
SShow or hide the scales
OShow or hide the circle of fifths
RTurn Reading mode on or off
EscClose the open dialog, menu or panel

Turn pages and navigate

These keys and mouse buttons turn pages and move through the app:

KeyAction
→ ↓ Page DownNext page, or scroll down in scroll mode
← ↑ Page UpPrevious page, or scroll up in scroll mode
Click the right or left half of the pageNext or previous page
Cmd [, Alt ← or Mouse 4Back
Cmd ], Alt → or Mouse 5Forward
Arrow keys and EnterIn the library, move between items and open a tab

Zoom and full screen

These shortcuts change the zoom and the screen mode:

KeyAction
Ctrl and the mouse wheel, or a pinchZoom the score between 50% and 150%
Mouse wheel in scroll modeScroll the score
Alt EnterTurn full screen on or off (Windows and Linux)
PreviousBulk export NextFoot pedals and game controllers
