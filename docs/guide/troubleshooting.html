Troubleshooting and questions

This page answers common questions and lists fixes for common problems. If your problem isn't here, open an issue on GitHub.

Common questions

Does MajesticTab work offline?

Yes. The built-in synth plays tabs without a connection. YouTube, web search, the music catalog and cloud sync need the internet. You choose whether to use them.

Does MajesticTab have subscriptions or locked features?

No. MajesticTab has no subscriptions, in-app purchases, ads or trial periods. On the Apple App Store, a one-time purchase unlocks the app on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Where does MajesticTab store my tabs?

On your device, unless you back them up or sync them. See Backup and sync.

How does MajesticTab help if I play a 7-string or 8-string guitar?

You can show a Guitar Pro tab on 7 or 8 strings, or move the frets to match your tuning, with a capo or transposed. Every note keeps its pitch. To start new files on 7 or 8 strings, set Default strings under Player in Settings. See Fit a tab to your guitar.

Fix sound that plays late

If you use a Bluetooth speaker or headphones, open Sound and set Audio latency, or select Calibrate. See Sound.

Fix a cursor that drifts from the recording

In the recording bar, select Edit sync. The Sync sheet shows which bars to check. Select Tap along again to tap over the bars where the cursor drifts, or select Edit points to nudge a single point. See Original recording.

Fix a wrong album cover or a missing tracklist

Open Settings, select Library and backup, and then select Reload next to Reload library metadata. Check your internet connection if the catalog doesn't respond.

Fix a pedal that doesn't turn pages

Check that the pedal is paired in your device's Bluetooth settings. If the pedal has a key setting, set it to send arrow keys. See Foot pedals and game controllers.

Fix a Jam that you can't join

Both devices must be on the same network. Some guest and public Wi-Fi networks stop devices from reaching each other. Use a private network or a phone hotspot instead.

Recover a library that disappeared in the browser

Browsers can clear site storage. Restore your library from a .mtbackup file. To prevent this, make regular backups or use cloud sync.

Report a problem

Open an issue on GitHub. Include what you did, what you expected, your platform and the app version.
