# The video sheet (YouTube player)

Read when touching `src/ytSheet.js`, `youtubePlayer.js`, the Original sound or anything that could hide, move or cover the player. Chrome around it: `reader.md`.

**The video sheet** (`src/ytSheet.js`, `#ytSheet`): the YouTube player is not in the Original panel. YouTube's developer policies forbid a background player (III.I.9: content played from a player "not displayed in the page, tab, or screen that the user is viewing") and its player docs set a 200 x 200 minimum viewport, so the player stays on the screen while it plays.
  - **When it exists.** While Original is the selected sound and its source is YouTube with a video known (a saved or synced one): it opens as soon as Original is selected, such as when a song with a recording opens, and does not wait for play (`scheduleOriginalVideo()`, called from `setActiveAudioMode` and when the reader comes back; opening a tab changes the sound several times, so the changes settle for 250ms and the sheet follows the last one, popping in once). It also opens when playback starts by any path (a press on play, Jam, a sync-started video). The Original panel is a menu like the others and opens over the sheet on every platform; both stay, and playing does not close the panel (an accepted overlap: the panel is the person's own and is dismissed with a press outside or Escape, and the guard ignores a reader panel over the player). A video picked or pasted while the panel is open plays as usual. A local file, the Synth, a new song, or leaving the reader closes it, and closing pauses. `closeSheet()` is the only way out and it pauses first. Never hide, shrink, move off-screen or cover a playing player: not with `display`, `visibility`, `opacity`, a 1px size, a position past the window edge, or any of our own controls (the one exception is the reader panel above). The guard in `youtubePlayer.js` restores the sheet if anything left a playing video hidden, and warns when the player is under 200 x 200 or covered.
  - **Layout.** Rows: grabber (and in tabletop the Video | Fretboard switch), then the video; no caption or Close button. Nothing is drawn over the video. The sheet overlaps the page: the score never reflows or shifts for it. Portrait phone: full width above the fretboard and the five words, and every sheet that opens (View, Practice, Sound, Guitar, Tools, Song) ends above it (`--yt-sheet-h` is the visible height). Desktop: at the right, above the dock, with the panels ending above it. Short landscape phone: a 356px column on the right with the right-hand panels beside it. The player is kept at YouTube's minimum viewport as a 16:9 player, 356 x 200, on every layout (never full width on a phone, never larger); the width falls to the screen's only if the screen is narrower. YouTube gives no way to read a video's real shape (oEmbed reports 200 x 113 for every video), so the player is not matched to the content, and album-art videos keep their bars. Do not crop the player to hide them.
  - **The peek.** The person (and only the person, by swiping the grabber down or pressing it, or arrow keys) pushes the sheet down to a peek: the top slice of the video (about 56px, its title and the YouTube mark) stays on the screen, over the page. This is a deliberate, accepted risk: the slice is smaller than the player docs' minimum, so nothing in the app may do it for the person (no timer, playback event or panel), and a new video or song opens the sheet fully again. The player keeps its full size inside the clip (the sheet is moved down and clipped at the bottom on web; `window.ytFit` anchors the player to the top of the native view), so its own viewport never shrinks.
  - **Native players (macOS, iOS).** The native view covers only the visible slice of the sheet (`visibleVideoRect()`); it is never parked off-screen or shrunk to a dot: when the sheet is not shown the player is paused and hidden. A native view draws above the page, so the reader panels (the Original panel included) that open over the sheet's rect are hidden behind the video where they overlap: check that in the real app. The other shells embed in the page (Windows, Android: direct iframe; Linux: the relay page `docs/youtube-player.html`) and need no special handling here. Check macOS, iOS, tabletop and the peek in the real app, since the browser cannot show the native view.
  - **Tabletop.** The sheet takes the fretboard's band. With Video chosen the fretboard steps aside; the Fretboard choice (the switch in the sheet's bar, shown only on Guitar Pro songs) lowers the sheet to its peek and brings the fretboard back above it. Panels end above the sheet. The same 356 x 200 player, centered.
  - **The sound panels on a portrait phone** (Synth, Original) are sheets like the other four words': they open from the Sound word and rise above the strip. When the video sheet is up they stack above it.
  - **Reading mode** leaves the video where it is: the sheet is part of playback, not chrome.
