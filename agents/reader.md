# Reader

Read when changing the reader chrome: the five words, the dock, the bar ruler, phone chrome or Reading mode. Related: `reader-video-sheet.md` (the YouTube player sheet), `reader-duo-fold.md` (folding iPhone). Visual rules: `ui-principles.md`.

- **The reader has five labelled buttons, the Song menu and one dock, the same words on every platform:** **View** (layout, notation, size, paper, pages shown, which tracks are drawn and Color by track; the Tracks group appears only when the file has more than one track to draw), **Practice** (speed, parts, loop range, metronome and count-in), **Sound** (the settings of the selected sound, Synth or Original, chosen by the switch at the top of the panel, see below), **Guitar** (strings, tuning and capo for the guitar in your hands: temporary, it resets when the song closes, and is saved only for the song or as the usual guitar when the player asks) and **Tools** (Chord library, Scales, Circle of fifths, Tuner, Metronome, Jam). The song's name in the top bar opens the **Song** menu (section marks, PDF options, save and share, and under Tabs a Score dropdown selector of the song's library tabs or pack scores including and indicating the current one; the selector is hidden when the song has no other tab). The dock holds the transport, the **Full band | Backing** switch and volume (on phones the volume lives in the sound panel). Full band | Backing is what you play, and it means the same under both sounds (`src/utils/soundSwitch.js`): with the Synth, Backing is the mix without the guitars (without the bass in bass mode; only a Guitar Pro file has the tracks, so it is off otherwise); with Original, Backing is the Backing slot, which for a set of stems is the same set with the guitars muted. Backing with nothing attached opens the Sound panel, where one is added. The switch only presses the controls that hold the state (the Synth track-mode radios, the slot rows), and the slot rows stay in the Original panel as hidden state. **Synth | Original** (where it comes from) is the switch at the top of both Sound panels, in place of a title; pressing it selects that sound and the open panel follows. The old dock buttons stay hidden, as the carriers of `active-audio-mode` that other code reads. The **Sound** word opens the panel of the selected sound and shows nothing else, so it never lists both. Both panels run the same four zones in the same order: the Synth | Original switch; the parts (Synth: Mixer with its Guitars only and Reset mix; Original with stems: Stems; Original otherwise: the recordings); set up (Synth: SoundFonts, a linked MIDI file; Original: Add audio file and Add a video, one field where a pasted link or video ID is added as it is and anything else is a search whose first match is added). The Original panel's recordings are two expandable sections, **Full band** and **Backing**, in the style of the other fold-away rows (`src/audioSync/slotRecordings.js`), whichever part the dock has selected. Each row names what that part plays now ("All stems", a video's title, "Nothing attached") and opens to the part's recordings: its videos, the audio files whose pack role is for it (backing role for Backing, every other role or none for Full band) and the file it plays now, with the audio in the Guitar Pro file under Full band. A row of the other part first makes that part the active one (the dock follows), then picks the recording. With a stem set both start closed so the stems stay on top; otherwise the active part's section is open, the dock changing part opens that part's, and with nothing attached both are open. Remove recordings… removes one listing, a video in one part: the same video in the other part is its own listing and stays (only a listing that shows map points, which belong to the video, takes the second press and deletes them). Add a video has one field and two buttons, **Add to Full band** and **Add to Backing**: nothing is added by Enter or a paste, with the field left as the app wrote it a button searches its own part's default (Backing's adds "guitar backing track"), and the buttons switch to their part and then add what was typed (read before the switch, because switching resets the field to that part's search). The old single lists keep rendering for the active slot as hidden state. There is no waveform, play button or time in the panel: the dock carries the transport; and last the output (Volume on phones, then Audio latency, which applies to every sound). Speed and sections are Practice only. The list of videos remembered for this recording is described below (switch with one press, each keeps its own sync points, Remove recordings…); the Sound panel holds no sync controls, see the Sync sheet below, and no source toggle: **Add audio file** and **Add a video** sit above one list, **Recordings for this tab** (`#realAudioRecordings`: the audio in the file, then audio files, then videos, always in that order; files first). Inside the audio files and inside the videos the order is by role first (`orderRecordings()` in `src/audioSync/recordingMeta.js`): original, alternate, other roles (live, solo, cover ...), backing, then recordings no pack described, newest first. A pack's order breaks ties inside a role (its first recording is its default, SPEC section 7), and a stem sits under the recording it was split from. What a pack says about each recording (role, label, place in the pack, track names of `scoreTracks`, stem parent) is kept by the import in `recordingMeta.js` (`rec_meta_<song>`, in backups); the row title is the pack's `label` when there is one, and the second line leads with the role (except original and backing) and the tracks ("Without Drums"). Keep it app-internal: export writes none of it. Pressing a row, or adding a file or a video, makes that kind the slot's sound through the `majestictab:use-source` event handled in `realAudioController.js`; `#ytDropdownPanel[data-rec-source]` says which kind is on, and the lists mark only that kind's selected row; the video itself is in the video sheet, `reader-video-sheet.md`). The list is `src/audioSync/videoHistory.js` (10 per slot, a video with sync points is never dropped, included in backups as `yt_videos_*`); a video found by search is remembered when played, one the person adds when added. With Original selected, a recording bar (time, track, sync state and one action: "Sync" with nothing set, "Edit sync" once points exist; on a Guitar Pro song both open the **Sync sheet**, below). A Guitar Pro recording with no sync points is followed loosely (`src/audioSync/looseFollow.js`): its length is laid over the score's (repeats expanded), the reading marker the text tabs use (`.reading-line`, no cursor) glides down the left edge of the score at that pace, sweeping through each line of music and on into the next rather than sitting on a staff group, with the page scrolling to keep it in view or turning at the end of the page, and the bar reads "Rough follow", shown as three plain roles: a status, **Rough follow**; **Earlier | Later**, one flat group that moves the marker a line at a time (disabled at the first and last line; the shift lasts until the recording is sought or changes, nothing is stored); and **Sync**, which replaces the estimate with an exact map. On phones they take a row of their own under the progress track, the status left, the group and Sync right, 44px tall sits above the bar ruler on Guitar Pro songs, and above the page strip on PDF and text tabs; audio latency applies to every sound and lives in the Sound panel; The fretboard sits above the dock on Guitar Pro songs, with no entry in Tools: a button in the dock (or the F key) toggles it. PDF and text tabs get a page strip in place of the bar ruler. Settings is the setonce place and holds the defaults that View (notation) and Guitar (usual guitar) override for one song. On phones see **Phone reader chrome** below. Do not add another place, and do not put the same control in two of them.
- **Stems** (`src/audioSync/stemSet.js`). A pack that is only stems (SPEC section 8, no original or backing, `stemSetOf()` in `mpak.js`) plays as one recording, "All stems", listed once; its parts are not listed as recordings. Both Recording and Backing play the same set (the lead stem's file, the one with the pack's sync points, which both share) and differ only in what starts muted: Recording hears every stem, Backing starts without the guitars (without the bass in bass mode). Under the recordings list, **Stems** (`#afStemSection`, shown only while a set is the sound) is built like the Synth panel's Mixer (`.track-mix` strips: name, M and S, Level; no pan): Mute, Solo (a second press brings every stem back) and Level per stem, and **Reset mix** back to the starting mix. The choice is kept per sound and song (`stem_mutes_*`), switching Recording | Backing keeps playing and only changes the mutes. The set rides on the lead's recording meta (`stems`), app-internal like the rest of it. A stem set always appears under both Full band and Backing, whichever recording each part plays, and the two differ only in what is muted: the Backing listing says how many stems are muted ("2 of 4 stems muted"), following the mix the person saved for it (`stem_mutes_*`, announced by `STEM_MUTES_EVENT`). **Add stems**, under Add audio file, makes a set from loose files (`src/audioSync/addStems.js`): a sheet lists the files with a name and an instrument for each (guessed from the file name, `stemGuess.js`; the instrument is what Backing starts without), the first file leads and keeps the map points, and the set is saved as the same "All stems" recording a stems-only pack makes and becomes the sound of both parts. A phone warns (and does not stop) above 8 stems; files that differ in length by more than a second get a warning. Every heard stem is decoded (about 20 MB a minute each), so a set of seven is heavy on a phone.
- **The bar ruler** (`src/gpProcessor/barRuler.js`) shows the song's sections as its own blocks, one level at a time (`buildSegments` in `songSections.js`): the file's numbered movements when it has them, otherwise its parts, and a block narrower than 24px joins its neighbour, so a narrow ruler shows fewer, larger blocks. Blocks have a 2px transparent gap and a played fill of their own. A block's name is the whole name, a cut at a word boundary, or for a movement its numeral; it is never a stray letter, and nothing is shown when nothing readable fits. The block the playhead is in says where it is to the part ("IV: The Darkest Of Winters · Riff V") in the room it has. The dock never names the section: the bar readout beside the ruler reserves room for the song's widest bar number (`--bar-digits`), so the ruler keeps one length through the song. While the playhead is moved (press, drag, arrow keys) a floating readout above it gives movement, section and bar. A file with no usable marks gets one plain block. Each block that is a known kind of section takes its family's hue (`sectionKind()` in `sectionKinds.js`: verse, chorus, bridge, solo, intro and outro, instrumental; it reads the words however the file wrote them, in several languages, and never guesses) on its ground and its played fill, at low chroma and the surface's own lightness (`--sec-*` in `design.css`, the same in every theme), so label contrast is that of the plain surface. The colour is never the only signal: the name is on the block, or in the scrub readout when the block is too narrow. Movements, merged blocks and names that say no kind stay plain.
- **Phone reader chrome** (`body.phone-reader`, set from `PHONE_READER_QUERY`: narrow screens and short landscape phones). The sheet never changes size on its own; it changes only when the player presses **Reading mode**. No timer, tap zone or playback event moves a bar.
  - **Top, one slim row over the score:** back, the song name (opens Song) and the page buttons. It overlays the score (in Scroll mode the score scrolls under it), so hiding it resizes nothing.
  - **Bottom, for the thumb (portrait):** the strip, then the five words (View, Practice, Sound, Guitar, Tools) as the last row above the home indicator. The strip has the progress track on top (bar ruler on Guitar Pro songs, page strip on PDF and text tabs, the recording bar under Original), then restart, play/pause, Full band | Backing, the fretboard toggle (Guitar Pro only) and the Reading mode toggle. The fretboard sits above the strip. Sheets rise from the bottom above the strip and the five words, short enough to leave the tab visible. Practice keeps speed, loop, metronome, count-in and parts. The five words are moved out of the top bar by `readerViewMenu.js` (a transformed bar would carry a fixed child with it).
  - **The top never hides on scroll.** Scrolling, timers, taps and playback leave every bar where it is; only Reading mode hides the top. In page mode, swiping left/right on the pages or tapping either side turns pages (swipe left or tap right half forward, swipe right or tap left half back).
  - **Page mode navigation & cursor deconfliction:** In page mode (`#output:not(.continuous-mode)` on GP, text, or PDF), gestures on the sheet are deconflicted with playback cursor positioning:
    - *Swipe vs Cursor:* Horizontal swipes ($\Delta x \ge 40\text{px}$, or $\ge 30\text{px}$ fast flick, $\lvert\Delta x\rvert > 1.25\lvert\Delta y\rvert$) turn pages (swipe left = NEXT, swipe right = PREV). If a swipe starts over a GP note, initial tick capture restores the tick position upon swipe finish so dragging across notation never displaces playback. Trailing clicks after a swipe are suppressed for 450ms.
    - *Taps vs Cursor:* On phone (`isPhoneReader()`), the whole score turns pages and taps never move the cursor ("no tap moves a bar"); scrubbing is done via the bar ruler. On desktop/tablet, clicking a specific GP beat (`api.beatMouseDown`) positions the cursor and suppresses page navigation (`__lastBeatClickTime`); clicking outside notation, on page gutters, margins, or text/PDF turns the page.
    - *Continuous Mode:* Page-turn taps and swipes are disabled; clicking anywhere on the score calculates the clicked beat and positions the cursor (`gpPlayer.js`).
  - **Reading mode** (the toggle in the bottom bar, on every platform, `R`, gamepad Y; `aria-pressed`; remembered on phones): hides the top, the five words, restart, the Full band | Backing switch, the fretboard button and the fretboard; keeps play/pause, the progress track and the toggle. One deliberate refit when it turns on and one when it turns off. Desktop works the same way (top bar away, bottom bar narrowed to play, progress and the toggle, no edge peek), but is not remembered. The toggle lives only in the bottom bar (View does not repeat it). `Z` is unused.
  - **Short landscape phone:** the five words stay in the top, which becomes a left rail (back, song name, the five words); Reading mode removes it and leaves the slim strip.
- **The Sync sheet** (`src/audioSync/syncSheet.js`, markup `#syncSheetModal`) is the one place to line a Guitar Pro recording up with the score. The dock's status action opens it; it states the sync state in words (the same `syncStateText` as the dock), names what is wrong (the flagged bar, bars with no point) and holds every way to get or fix sync: **Tap along** (the one primary action), **Edit points** (the point editor), **Use another recording's sync** (borrowing from another video or file of the song, with a second press to replace existing points), **Use another tab's sync**, **Use the original recording's sync** (backing slot only) and **Import points…**. It drives tap mode, the editor and `borrowFrom` (`audioSyncUi.js`) rather than copying them, so the TV and Jam-guest rules stay in one place. Do not add sync controls to the Sound panel or the tap bar again. Text and PDF tabs keep their own bar: **Sync** there starts tapping.
- **The tap bar** (`#syncModeBar`) keeps what tapping needs in one row (play, back 5 and 10 seconds, speed, Undo, the count, **More**, and **Done** above). Fix range, Offset, Calibrate, Snap, Redo, Timeline and Edit points sit behind **More** (`#smMore`, closed each time tap mode starts). Do not put a new tool in the first row. **Done** leaves tap mode and opens the Sync sheet again (`openSyncSheetFor`).
