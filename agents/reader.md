# Reader

Read when changing the reader chrome: the five words, the dock, the bar ruler, phone chrome or Reading mode. Related: `reader-video-sheet.md` (the YouTube player sheet), `reader-duo-fold.md` (folding iPhone). Visual rules: `ui-principles.md`.

- **The reader has five labelled buttons, the Song menu and one dock, the same words on every platform:** **View** (layout, notation, size, paper, pages shown, which tracks are drawn and Color by track; the Tracks group appears only when the file has more than one track to draw), **Practice** (speed, parts, loop range, metronome and count-in), **Sound** (the settings of the sound selected in the dock: Synth audio or Original audio, see below), **Guitar** (strings, tuning and capo for the guitar in your hands: temporary, it resets when the song closes, and is saved only for the song or as the usual guitar when the player asks) and **Tools** (Chord library, Scales, Circle of fifths, Tuner, Metronome, Jam). The song's name in the top bar opens the **Song** menu (section marks, PDF options, save and share). The dock holds the transport, the Synth | Original switch and volume (on phones the volume lives in the sound panel). Pressing Synth or Original in the dock only selects that sound (Original with nothing set up yet opens its settings, since there is nothing to play until a video or file is added; with the Sound panel open, the panel follows the selection). The **Sound** word opens the panel of the selected sound and shows nothing else, so it never lists both (Synth: what you hear and the SoundFont; Original: the source, with Recording | Backing, then Add a video (one field: a pasted link or video ID is added as it is, anything else is a search whose first match is added), the list of videos remembered for this recording (switch with one press, each keeps its own sync points, copy sync points between videos, Remove videos…) and sync points; the video itself is in the video sheet, `reader-video-sheet.md`). The list is `src/audioSync/videoHistory.js` (10 per slot, a video with sync points is never dropped, included in backups as `yt_videos_*`); a video found by search is remembered when played, one the person adds when added. With Original selected, a recording bar (time, track, sync state and "Add points") sits above the bar ruler on Guitar Pro songs, and above the page strip on PDF and text tabs; audio latency applies to every sound and lives in the Sound panel; The fretboard sits above the dock on Guitar Pro songs, with no entry in Tools: a button in the dock (or the F key) toggles it. PDF and text tabs get a page strip in place of the bar ruler. Settings is the setonce place and holds the defaults that View (notation) and Guitar (usual guitar) override for one song. On phones see **Phone reader chrome** below. Do not add another place, and do not put the same control in two of them.
- **The bar ruler** (`src/gpProcessor/barRuler.js`) shows the song's sections as its own blocks, one level at a time (`buildSegments` in `songSections.js`): the file's numbered movements when it has them, otherwise its parts, and a block narrower than 24px joins its neighbour, so a narrow ruler shows fewer, larger blocks. Blocks have a 2px transparent gap and a played fill of their own. A block's name is the whole name, a cut at a word boundary, or for a movement its numeral; it is never a stray letter, and nothing is shown when nothing readable fits. The block the playhead is in says where it is to the part ("IV: The Darkest Of Winters · Riff V") in the room it has. The dock never names the section: the bar readout beside the ruler reserves room for the song's widest bar number (`--bar-digits`), so the ruler keeps one length through the song. While the playhead is moved (press, drag, arrow keys) a floating readout above it gives movement, section and bar. A file with no usable marks gets one plain block. Each block that is a known kind of section takes its family's hue (`sectionKind()` in `sectionKinds.js`: verse, chorus, bridge, solo, intro and outro, instrumental; it reads the words however the file wrote them, in several languages, and never guesses) on its ground and its played fill, at low chroma and the surface's own lightness (`--sec-*` in `design.css`, the same in every theme), so label contrast is that of the plain surface. The colour is never the only signal: the name is on the block, or in the scrub readout when the block is too narrow. Movements, merged blocks and names that say no kind stay plain.
- **Phone reader chrome** (`body.phone-reader`, set from `PHONE_READER_QUERY`: narrow screens and short landscape phones). The sheet never changes size on its own; it changes only when the player presses **Reading mode**. No timer, tap zone or playback event moves a bar.
  - **Top, one slim row over the score:** back, the song name (opens Song) and the page buttons. It overlays the score (in Scroll mode the score scrolls under it), so hiding it resizes nothing.
  - **Bottom, for the thumb (portrait):** the strip, then the five words (View, Practice, Sound, Guitar, Tools) as the last row above the home indicator. The strip has the progress track on top (bar ruler on Guitar Pro songs, page strip on PDF and text tabs, the recording bar under Original), then restart, play/pause, Synth | Original, the fretboard toggle (Guitar Pro only) and the Reading mode toggle. The fretboard sits above the strip. Sheets rise from the bottom above the strip and the five words, short enough to leave the tab visible. Practice keeps speed, loop, metronome, count-in and parts. The five words are moved out of the top bar by `readerViewMenu.js` (a transformed bar would carry a fixed child with it).
  - **The top never hides on scroll.** Scrolling, timers, taps and playback leave every bar where it is; only Reading mode hides the top. In page mode, swiping left/right on the pages or tapping either side turns pages (swipe left or tap right half forward, swipe right or tap left half back).
  - **Page mode navigation & cursor deconfliction:** In page mode (`#output:not(.continuous-mode)` on GP, text, or PDF), gestures on the sheet are deconflicted with playback cursor positioning:
    - *Swipe vs Cursor:* Horizontal swipes ($\Delta x \ge 40\text{px}$, or $\ge 30\text{px}$ fast flick, $\lvert\Delta x\rvert > 1.25\lvert\Delta y\rvert$) turn pages (swipe left = NEXT, swipe right = PREV). If a swipe starts over a GP note, initial tick capture restores the tick position upon swipe finish so dragging across notation never displaces playback. Trailing clicks after a swipe are suppressed for 450ms.
    - *Taps vs Cursor:* On phone (`isPhoneReader()`), the whole score turns pages and taps never move the cursor ("no tap moves a bar"); scrubbing is done via the bar ruler. On desktop/tablet, clicking a specific GP beat (`api.beatMouseDown`) positions the cursor and suppresses page navigation (`__lastBeatClickTime`); clicking outside notation, on page gutters, margins, or text/PDF turns the page.
    - *Continuous Mode:* Page-turn taps and swipes are disabled; clicking anywhere on the score calculates the clicked beat and positions the cursor (`gpPlayer.js`).
  - **Reading mode** (the toggle in the bottom bar, on every platform, `R`, gamepad Y; `aria-pressed`; remembered on phones): hides the top, the five words, restart, the Synth | Original switch, the fretboard button and the fretboard; keeps play/pause, the progress track and the toggle. One deliberate refit when it turns on and one when it turns off. Desktop works the same way (top bar away, bottom bar narrowed to play, progress and the toggle, no edge peek), but is not remembered. The toggle lives only in the bottom bar (View does not repeat it). `Z` is unused.
  - **Short landscape phone:** the five words stay in the top, which becomes a left rail (back, song name, the five words); Reading mode removes it and leaves the slim strip.
